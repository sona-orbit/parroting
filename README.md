# Parroting (學舌鳥) — voice typing and dictation for Mac and iPhone that learns your words

Parroting is a voice typing app: hold a key, speak, and the text is pasted into whatever app you are using. Speech recognition runs on your Mac, punctuation and spacing are tidied for you, and over time it learns your names, terms and tone. The Mac app is a free download for Apple silicon; on iPhone, Parroting is a keyboard that works in every app.

## Features

- **Hold, speak, release**: hold the shortcut in your current app and the text is pasted at the cursor. Tap once for continuous dictation.
- **On-device speech recognition**: recording and recognition happen on your Mac. Pick a speech model during setup and switch later in Settings.
- **Clean text automatically**: punctuation and spacing are handled; optional AI cleanup removes fillers and false starts. Chinese typesetting and script conversion settings appear when you use Chinese.
- **Personal dictionary and history**: save names and terms, correct misheard words, and revisit previous dictation.
- **On-device tone reminders** for Chinese, English and Japanese, even mixed in one message, flagging words that may hurt before you send.
- **Rewrite modes**: tone, organize, translate, meeting notes and email, using the AI service you choose with your own key. Local features work without a key.
- **Personalized learning**: review word corrections and tone feedback, train on your Mac, then adopt the result or roll back. Training data stays on your Mac.
- **Mac and iPhone together**: the iPhone keyboard shares your dictionary with the Mac through an app-specific folder in your own iCloud.
