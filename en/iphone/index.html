FAQ

Is “Allow Full Access” safe?

It’s the name iOS gives every keyboard that needs to talk to its main app; when you turn it on, iOS warns that it can transmit what you type. Parroting has no account, ads or tracking; your voice is recognized on the phone and never sent to us. Only if you choose “My API key” is the text to rewrite sent directly to the AI service you picked.

Is there a subscription?

No. Pay once, and all future updates are included. With your own API key, the AI service bills you directly (Gemini has a free tier).

How does it relate to the Mac app?

The Mac app is free. With iCloud Drive on, your dictionary, rewrite styles and history are shared between Mac and iPhone.
