FAQ

Is Allow Full Access safe?

It's the iOS name for the permission every keyboard needs to talk to its app; iOS warns that it can transmit what you type. Parroting has no account, ads or tracking, and your voice is recognised on your iPhone, never sent to us. Only with your own API key does the text you rewrite go straight to the AI provider you chose.

Is there a subscription?

No. Buy once; updates are included. With your own API key, the AI provider bills you directly (Gemini has a free tier).

How does it relate to the Mac app?

The Mac app is free. With iCloud Drive on, the dictionary, rewrite styles and history are shared between Mac and iPhone.

Which iPhone do I need?

Any iPhone on iOS 26 or later. Apple's built-in AI needs an iPhone that supports Apple Intelligence (iPhone 15 Pro or later).
