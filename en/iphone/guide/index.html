How to use Parroting on iPhone

From turning on the keyboard to speaking and pasting, in ten minutes. The Mac guide is here.

  1. 1

    Open Parroting once

    After installing, open the Parroting app. The first run asks for the microphone, has you try a sentence, then turns on the keyboard. Follow it through and you are ready.

  2. 2

    Turn on the Parroting keyboard

    In iPhone Settings, go to General → Keyboard → Keyboards → Add New Keyboard and pick Parroting. Then tap Parroting and turn on Allow Full Access. The keyboard needs it to talk to the app and put text in the field; what you type is never collected. You can also follow the steps in Parroting under Settings → Turn on the Parroting keyboard.

  3. 3

    Speak to type

    In any text field, switch to Parroting with the globe key and tap the green mic. The first time, it jumps to Parroting to open the microphone; when you finish, go back to your app. Within the standby time you can tap again without jumping. Tap Done and the text lands in the field.

  4. 4

    The keys

    ↶ ↷ undo and redo what was just pasted; ⌫ deletes; there are @ and Return. The bottom row holds punctuation: swipe it for more symbols, switch full-/half-width, and use ‹ › to move the cursor. You can also slide across a blank part of the keyboard to move the cursor.

  5. 5

    AI rewrite formats

    Pick a format at the top right of the keyboard, such as Tone fix, Email or Organized, and every dictation is rewritten before it is pasted. Tap the same format again to turn it off. Edit formats in Settings → Edit rewrite formats.

  6. 6

    Rewrite

    Select the text to change, or the take you just said, and open the Rewrite tab. Set the Length, Formality, Tone and Structure sliders, then tap Rewrite: the result replaces the text in the field. To adjust, set the sliders and tap Rewrite again; tap ↶ to get the original back.

  7. 7

    Translate

    Open the Translate tab and tap a language: the translation replaces the text in the field, and ↶ brings the original back. Choose your four languages in Settings → Translation languages.

  8. 8

    Dictionary and suggestions

    Add names, products and terms Parroting keeps getting wrong to the Dictionary, and recognition and the AI will prefer your spelling. Parroting also notices what you fix in the same field after a paste; when the same mishearing is fixed twice it becomes a suggestion, and at ten Home reminds you so you can add them in one tap.

  9. 9

    Settings

    AI source: Apple on-device, or your own API key. Recognition language follows the system and can be changed when you speak another language. Report a problem from Settings if something goes wrong.
