Just once

First-time setup

The first time you open Parroting, it walks you through these same three steps.

1

Download it and open Parroting once

It asks for the microphone, has you try a sentence, then turns on the keyboard. Follow along and you’re set.

2

Turn on the Parroting keyboard

In Parroting, tap “Go to Settings”. It opens Parroting’s own page in iPhone Settings; tap “Keyboards”.

Go to Settings
↓
Settings › Parroting
Keyboards ›

Or by hand: Settings › General › Keyboard › Keyboards › Add New Keyboard › Parroting.

3

Turn on “Allow Full Access”

On the “Keyboards” page, turn on both “Parroting” and “Allow Full Access”. The keyboard needs it to talk to the app and put text in the field; what you type is never collected.

The keys

Parroting is voice only; it has no letter keys. To type, tap the globe key to switch back to an Apple keyboard.

⇠ ⇢

Voice / Rewrite / Translate　Three pages; tap to switch.

AI Rewrite　Pick a format (Tone fix, Email, Organized…) and a colored button joins “Done” while you talk: it rewrites in that format, then pastes; “Done” pastes your words as said. Tap the same format again to turn it off.

↶ ↷　Undo or redo what was just pasted.

⌫　@　Return　Delete, type @, start a new line.

Punctuation　Swipe sideways for more symbols.

全 / 半　Switches between full-width and half-width punctuation.

‹ ›　Move the cursor one character left or right.

Swipe　Swipe left or right on any blank part of the keyboard to move the cursor too.

Text you’ve already written

Rewrite & translate

The result replaces the text in the field; there’s no separate preview.

Rewrite

Set the sliders, tap “Rewrite”

  1. 1Select the text to change (or the take you just said) and switch to the “Rewrite” page.
  2. 2Set the four sliders: length, formality, tone and structure.
  3. 3Tap the big “Rewrite” button below; the result replaces the text in the field.

Moving a slider changes nothing until you tap “Rewrite”. To adjust, set the sliders and tap it again; not happy, tap ↶ to get the original back.

If you get stuck

Most problems are one of these four.

Parroting isn’t there when you tap the globe key

Go back to step 2 of First-time setup and check that Parroting was added under “Add New Keyboard”.

The mic does nothing, or keeps jumping to the app

Check that “Allow Full Access” is on. The first tap jumping to Parroting is normal: tap “◀” at the top left to go back and keep talking. Within the standby time it won’t jump again. Set it in Parroting under Settings › Standby; with “Off” it jumps every time.

Password fields switch to the Apple keyboard

That’s an iOS rule for every third-party keyboard.

The bird shows “Problem”

An orange line on the keyboard says what’s wrong (for example a wrong API key or a used-up quota). Tap it to open Parroting’s AI settings and fix it there.
