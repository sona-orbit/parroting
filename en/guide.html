Guide

Learn Parroting in five minutes

From the first time you speak to the point where it really gets you. Here is every basic you will need.

Before you start

First launch
Parroting walks you through allowing Microphone and Accessibility in System Settings. The microphone records your voice; Accessibility lets Parroting paste text into the app you are using.
First launch after an update
The first time you open Parroting after each update, the speech model takes about 20 seconds to get ready. After that there is no wait.
Privacy
Parroting does not read the contents of password fields or apps such as online banking.

Speak

Hold to talk, release to paste
Right ⌥＋Right ⌘Hold and speak. When you let go, the text is pasted right where your cursor is.
Tap once for continuous dictation
Turn on “Continuous dictation” on the Speak page. Then a quick tap of the shortcut (released within 0.3 seconds) keeps Parroting listening, and each pause of about 1 second pastes a segment (you can set this to 1–3 seconds). Tap again to stop. Holding for more than 0.3 seconds works as normal hold to talk.
Cancel
EscCancel at any time. In continuous dictation, segments that were already cut can be found again under “Recover recordings”.
New line after each paste
Turn this on from the Home page in Settings and each segment ends with a line break. It never presses send for you.
External keyboards
If your keyboard has no right-hand ⌥ or ⌘, pick another combination in Settings → Speak.

AI cleanup

Drops filler words and false starts
With “AI cleanup” turned on from the Home page in Settings, saying “Um, let’s meet at three, no wait, four” pastes “Let’s meet at 4.” Numbers, names and negatives are never changed.
Without an AI key
Parroting falls back to on-device rules that remove standalone fillers like “um” and “uh” and repeated words, so “I I’ll go tomorrow” becomes “I’ll go tomorrow.”

Rewrite

The “✦ Rewrite” button
After each paste, “✦ Rewrite” appears next to the overlay for about 4 seconds. Click it to open the rewrite panel. It does not appear when you are talking to an AI, when the text is shorter than 4 characters, or when there is no AI key.
Always show the rewrite button
Too quick to catch? Turn on “Always show the rewrite button” from the Home page in Settings and the button stays until you start speaking again or click × to close it.
Pick a format
At the top of the panel, choose Email, Meeting notes, Translate, or one of your own formats. Click it again to deselect. Each app remembers the format you used last, so Mail picks Email automatically, for example.
Adjust the tone
Use the Warmth and Formality sliders.
Replace
Click “Replace” to swap out the text you just pasted. Not happy with it? Press ⌘Z to undo.
My formats
Go to AI rewrite → Rewrite formats → Add format, give it a name and describe what the AI should write. You can edit the built-in formats too, and restore the defaults at any time.
Translate
The direction is detected for you: Chinese becomes English, and English becomes Chinese.

Tone reminders

The reminder card
If something you are about to send could hurt, a reminder card pops up and the rewrite panel opens with it. You can rewrite, or send it as it is.
Wrong call
If you think the reminder was not needed, click “Wrong call”. This feedback adds up and is used to train judgment that is yours.
No hints in this app
One click on the reminder card adds the current app to the no-reminder list. You can remove it later under “Where to remind”.
Where to remind
In Tone & context → Where to remind, add the apps where you never want reminders. You can also switch reminders off by scene; chats with an AI are quiet by default.
A gentle nudge when you are too casual
When the situation calls for formality but you sound too casual, a quiet hint appears in the corner without interrupting you.

Dictionary

Added only when you confirm
Words you correct are not added to the dictionary automatically. They first go on the review list in Personalization, for example “Before: Catherine / After: Kathryn”, and are only added when you click confirm. That way, an edit where you simply chose different wording is not mistaken for a recognition error.
Add your own
Go to Words & history → Add and enter the correct spelling and the ways it is commonly misheard.
Correct a few more times at first
Speech recognition uses the fastest on-device model by default, which occasionally mishears sound-alike words or English terms. Correct it a few more times at the start and your common words will soon come out right. You can also switch to a larger model in Settings.
Manage your dictionary
Search, sort and delete in bulk, and import or export CSV.

Personal training

Where to find it
Personalization in the sidebar.
Start training
Once you have confirmed 20 pieces of tone feedback, you can click “Start training”. Training runs on your own Mac, and your data is not uploaded.
Check, then adopt
When training finishes you first see the results: which sentences are now judged differently. If you are happy with them, click “Adopt”. You can go back to the previous version at any time.

AI service

Where to set it up
On the AI rewrite page, choose an AI service and enter your API key. The key is stored in the macOS keychain.
Without a key
AI cleanup falls back to on-device rules, and rewriting is unavailable. Tone reminders, the dictionary and on-device speech recognition all keep working.
Getting a Gemini key: 1. Open the page
In your browser, open aistudio.google.com/api-keys and sign in with your Google account. The first time, you need to accept the terms of service. Going straight to this address is quickest; the entry point is easy to miss from the AI Studio home page.
2. Create a key
Click “Create API key” in the top-right corner, leave the name and project as they are, then click “Create key”. A string starting with AIza appears; click the copy icon next to it.
3. Paste it into Parroting
On the AI rewrite page, choose Gemini as the AI service and paste the key you just copied into the API key field. If you forget it later, no problem: you can copy it again from the same page at any time.
4. Limits and cost (optional)
The free tier has per-minute and per-day request limits, which are usually enough for everyday dictation and rewriting. If you use it heavily, add a credit card under Billing in the AI Studio sidebar to raise the limits. Google sets a monthly spending cap on newly linked accounts, so a large bill will not appear out of nowhere.

Overlay

Move it
Drag the overlay anywhere you like, and Parroting remembers where you put it. To put it back, click “Back to default position” in Tone & context.
