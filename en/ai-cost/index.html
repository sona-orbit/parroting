What does your own API key cost?

With everyday use, most services cost under US$1 a month, and Gemini, Groq and Zhipu GLM have free tiers.

Other things that use AI

  • Rewrite, translate and compare formats: one call each, about the same as tidying a sentence.
  • Tone hints: judged on the phone at no cost; AI is asked for a gentler wording only when a hint shows.
  • Fun reports: once a week and once a month, and only after you agree to send your history to the AI.

Free tiers

Free tiers limit requests per minute and per day. Past the limit, Parroting pastes the text tidied on the device, so typing is never blocked. Limits usually reset the next day.

Avoiding surprises

Most services let you set a spending limit or a usage alert in your account. Your API key stays on your device, and text goes straight from your device to the service you pick, never through Parroting.

Without an API key

On iPhone 15 Pro and later you can pick Apple built-in, which uses Apple Intelligence on the phone: free, and the text never leaves the phone.
