superwhisper in Chinese: Language, Models, Traditional Output

superwhisper supports Chinese, but its default language is Automatic. When you speak Chinese, it sometimes turns the text into English. The wrong model can also be slow and inaccurate. This article follows the official superwhisper docs and changelog. It turns the three things you need to adjust for Chinese into steps: language, voice model, and Traditional output. At the end you get a mixed-language setup and some alternatives to compare.

I'm Sona Orbit, the indie developer who builds Parroting on my own. Parroting is also a Mac dictation app, similar to superwhisper, so I use it for the comparison in section 6. For superwhisper's features, models, and pricing, I only write what its official sources say (voice models, troubleshooting wrong language detection, mode settings, changelog). I checked them on November 17, 2026, when the latest Mac version was 3.3.1. Menu names use the English wording from the official docs. Your screen may look slightly different. For Parroting, I use version 0.4.20.

1. The short answer: three places to adjust for Chinese

  1. Language. Edit the mode you use and change Language from Automatic to Chinese. The official docs call this the most reliable fix for “speaking Chinese but getting English.”
  2. Voice model. The free plan uses the Whisper family. One of them is a Chinese-only model, Whisper Large v3 Turbo (Chinese). The Pro plan adds Cohere Transcribe, a local model that supports Chinese, and the cloud model S1-Voice.
  3. Traditional characters. The changelog mentions an experimental Traditional Chinese option for Whisper models. If you still get Simplified characters, ask for Taiwan Traditional Chinese in the AI instructions of a custom mode.

When you finish, click into any text field and press ⌥ Space (Option + Space) to say a sentence. The details for each step are below.

2. Change the mode's language to Chinese

superwhisper settings work per mode. Each mode has its own language, voice model, and AI processing. So you change the language to Chinese in the mode you actually use.

  1. Open superwhisper's settings and click Modes in the sidebar.
  2. Click the mode you use. The active mode has a green dot in the list.
  3. In the language setting under Voice processing, change Automatic to Chinese.

The official troubleshooting page says that when the language is Automatic, non-English dictation sometimes comes out as English text. The model can't tell which language you are speaking, so it falls back to English. This happens at the transcription stage, before AI processing. To check whether this is your problem, open History. It shows the raw transcript from before AI processing.

The languages you can choose depend on the voice model the mode uses. When you change the model, the language list changes too. Check it again afterward to be safe.

If you switch between Chinese and English often, the official advice is to make two modes, one set to Chinese and one set to English, and switch when needed. The shortcut for switching modes is ⌥ ⇧ K (Option + Shift + K). You can also use Activate for apps to apply a specific mode automatically in certain apps.

Each mode's edit screen has a Voice Model menu. Local models must be downloaded first in Settings → Models library. The table below lists only the models the official docs say support Chinese.

Here is how to choose in a few common situations.

  • On the free plan and mostly speaking Chinese: try Whisper Large v3 Turbo (Chinese) first. Then compare it with the regular Large v3 Turbo. Say a few of your usual sentences with each.
  • Already on Pro and want to stay local: the official docs list Cohere Transcribe as the most accurate local model. Its error rate in the official tests is 8.4%. That is the overall result across the official eight test sets, not a Chinese-only number.
  • Fine with the cloud and want the least effort: S1-Voice recognizes speech in the cloud, so there is nothing to download. The docs say cloud models follow a zero data retention policy.

With local models, your voice does not leave your device. After one download, they work without a network connection. The docs describe local models as fast on modern hardware.

On price, the official site lists Pro monthly billing at $8.49 per month. For yearly and lifetime pricing, check the official site. The free plan includes unlimited use of Whisper models.

4. Getting Traditional characters: the experimental option and AI processing

This is where users in Taiwan get stuck most often. superwhisper's changelog has two entries about Traditional Chinese.

  • Version 1.45.9 (April 15, 2025) added an experimental Traditional Chinese language option for Whisper models.
  • Version 1.46.0 (May 27, 2025) added language support for English (British) and Chinese (Traditional).

The current official docs do not give the exact name of this option in the list. So look through the mode's language list for the Traditional Chinese entry. It sits under the Whisper models. If you switch to another model, you may not see it.

If Simplified characters still show up now and then after you pick it, you can patch it with the mode's AI processing. Create a Custom mode and write this in the AI instructions: “Always output Traditional Chinese as used in Taiwan; do not translate or change the meaning.” The official docs mention that a custom mode can include requirements such as keeping the input language in its AI instructions. Note that AI processing uses a language model. Check the official site for which language models each plan can use.

5. Setting up mixed Chinese and English

People in Taiwan often mix English words into Chinese sentences, such as ‘this bug 我明天 fix’. That is a Chinese sentence with English words in it, meaning “I'll fix this bug tomorrow.” For this case, the official docs give two paths.

  • If you speak one language most of the time, fix the language to Chinese. This is more stable than Automatic.
  • If you often mix two languages in one dictation, that is the one case where the docs recommend Automatic. To keep it from always guessing English, go to Settings → Vocabulary and add a hint. Write the hint in Chinese, with the meaning “it may also include Chinese words.”

The docs also say to keep the Vocabulary list short. Adding too many words can make transcription worse. How well the hint works varies by model. If it does not help, switch back to a fixed language.

The most reliable way to decide is to test. Take the three to five mixed-language sentences you say most often in a day. Say each a few times with both settings and see which one makes fewer mistakes.

6. Other options to compare

superwhisper is available on Mac, Windows, iOS, and Android, and it has many models to choose from. If you only use a Mac and mostly speak Chinese, these two are also worth comparing.

  • Built-in macOS dictation: free, nothing to install, and it supports Mandarin (Taiwan). But in Apple's feature list, Mandarin (Taiwan) is not among the languages for on-device dictation, so assume it will use the network. I cover how to turn it on in How to turn on voice typing on a Mac.
  • Parroting: the free Mac dictation app I build. Hold Right ⌥ + Right ⌘ and speak. When you let go, the text is pasted at the cursor. Speech is recognized locally on your Mac, and you can choose models such as SenseVoice, Qwen3-ASR, and Breeze-ASR. “Settings → Talk” has a “Chinese script” option that fixes the output to Traditional or Simplified. AI-rewritten results follow the same setting.

7. FAQ

Does superwhisper support Chinese?

Yes. The official docs say the multilingual Whisper models and the cloud model S1-Voice support more than 100 languages. The local Cohere Transcribe supports 14 languages, and Chinese is one of them. There is also a Chinese-only model, Whisper Large v3 Turbo (Chinese).

Why does superwhisper turn my Chinese into English?

Usually the language is set to Automatic and the model guesses the wrong language. The official advice is to edit the mode and change Language to Chinese. If you must use Automatic, add a hint in Settings → Vocabulary, written in Chinese, saying that Chinese words may also appear.

Can superwhisper output Traditional Chinese?

The changelog says that in April and May 2025, a Traditional Chinese language option was added for Whisper models, and it was labeled experimental at the time. If you still get Simplified, ask for Taiwan Traditional Chinese in the AI instructions of a Custom mode.

Can the free version use Chinese?

Yes. The official site says the free plan includes unlimited use of Whisper models. That includes the Chinese-only Whisper Large v3 Turbo (Chinese). Cohere Transcribe and the cloud models need Pro.

Does superwhisper upload my voice for Chinese recognition?

It depends on the model you choose. The official docs say that with local models your voice does not leave your device and they work offline. Cloud models process audio on servers and follow a zero data retention policy.
