Keep the rewrite panel open: every sentence comes out the way you want

Pick a format once and it applies to every sentence. With Auto replace on, it keeps working when you close the panel: per sentence pastes at each pause; whole-piece waits for Done.

What it is for

Three common uses.

Always translate before pasting

When you chat with a foreign client, say it in Chinese and paste English. The direction is picked for you: Chinese, Japanese or Korean becomes English, and English becomes Chinese.

Think out loud, get tidy text

Use Organize. It is fine to ramble: the point comes first, repeats and abandoned versions are dropped, and every fact you mentioned stays.

How it works

Five steps.

1

Open the rewrite panel

After you dictate something, click “✦ Rewrite” next to the overlay. Or go to Settings → Home and turn on “Keep the rewrite panel open”, and the panel appears.

“✦ Rewrite” stays for about 4 seconds after a paste. Press it before you have dictated anything and it opens an empty panel, ready for your next sentence.

4

Turn on “Auto replace”

Per sentence (Tone, Organize, Translate) rewrites and pastes at every pause. Whole (Meeting notes, Email, and custom formats by default) collects captions without pasting while you speak; press Done to paste the whole piece at once. Only the rewrite is pasted.

Change each format in AI rewrite → Rewrite formats → When to process.

Good to know

A few things to keep in mind.

  • The format still works with the panel closed× or Esc turns Keep the rewrite panel open off; with Auto replace on, the chosen format keeps working. The recording pill shows a green “✦ Organize” tag beside “Continuous”; click it to open the panel, change the format or turn Auto replace off. Your choice survives restarts.
  • Meeting notes never invent detailsA topic line and bullets of what you said; decisions, to-dos, times, people, owners and deadlines appear only if you said them. No format adds details you did not say.
  • The panel stays after “Replace”It follows your next sentence, so there is nothing to reopen.
  • It needs an AI serviceNo key yet? See Get a free Gemini API key. Without an AI service there is no rewrite panel, and tone hints still show on the overlay. If the AI service stops working, the panel closes and the switch turns off.
  • Each app remembers its formatEach app remembers the format you used last, so it is already picked the next time you speak there.

Your own format

Make a format of your own.

Make a format of your own

In AI rewrite → Rewrite formats, click Add format, name it and describe how to write. Set When to process to Per sentence or Whole (the default for your formats). No format adds details you did not say.

You can edit the built-in formats too, and click “Restore default” any time to bring them back.
