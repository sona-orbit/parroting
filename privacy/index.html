隱私權政策

適用於學舌鳥 iPhone 版（含學舌鳥鍵盤）・生效日 2026-10-31

學舌鳥不需要帳號，沒有廣告、沒有分析或追蹤工具，我們也沒有自己的伺服器。你的資料不會傳給我們。

聲音與語音辨識

你說的話由 iPhone 內建的語音辨識在手機上轉成文字。錄音不會存檔，也不會上傳。

學舌鳥鍵盤與「允許完全取用」

鍵盤需要「允許完全取用」，才能請學舌鳥 App 開麥克風、把辨識好的文字放回輸入框，並在你使用改寫或翻譯時連上你選的 AI 服務。鍵盤不記錄你打的字。只有你按下改寫或翻譯時，鍵盤才會讀取輸入框裡的文字，並把它送去處理。

AI 功能

AI 整理口語、AI 改寫、翻譯與語氣提醒的替代說法，使用你在設定裡選的 AI 來源：

  • Apple 內建：由 iPhone 上的 Apple Intelligence 處理，文字不離開手機。
  • 我的 API key：文字從你的手機直接送到你自己設定的 AI 服務（例如 Google Gemini、OpenAI），不經過我們。這些服務依各自的隱私權政策處理資料。API key 存在 iPhone 的鑰匙圈裡。

語氣提醒的判斷由內建在 App 裡的小模型在手機上完成。

文字紀錄、詞典與設定

文字紀錄、個人詞典、改寫格式與「我的資料」存在你的手機上。如果你開了 iCloud 雲碟，這些資料也會存進你自己的 iCloud，讓你的 Mac 版學舌鳥一起使用。我們看不到你的 iCloud。你可以隨時在 App 裡刪除紀錄，或刪除 App 清掉所有資料。

兒童

本 App 不是為兒童設計的，我們也不會蒐集兒童的資料。

本網站

本網站（sona-orbit.github.io）使用 Google Analytics 統計造訪人數，以及哪些頁面有人閱讀。Google Analytics 會使用 Cookie，並收到你瀏覽的頁面、瀏覽器與裝置類型，以及從 IP 位址推算的大概位置。這只適用於網站，App 本身不包含 Google Analytics。你可以在瀏覽器的隱私設定中封鎖，或安裝 Google 的停用外掛：tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout。

Privacy Policy

Parroting for iPhone (including the Parroting keyboard) · Effective 2026-10-31

Parroting needs no account and has no ads, analytics or tracking. We run no servers, and none of your data is sent to us.

Voice. Speech is turned into text on your iPhone by Apple's built-in speech recognition. Recordings are not saved or uploaded.

Keyboard and Full Access. Full Access lets the keyboard ask the Parroting app to use the microphone, put the recognized text back into the field, and reach the AI service you chose when you use Rewrite or Translate. The keyboard does not log what you type; it reads the text in the field only when you tap Rewrite or Translate.

AI features. Cleanup, AI Rewrite, Translate and tone suggestions use the AI source you pick in Settings. With Apple built-in, Apple Intelligence processes the text on your iPhone. With My API key, the text goes from your iPhone directly to the AI provider you set up (for example Google Gemini or OpenAI), never through us, and that provider's privacy policy applies. Your API key is kept in the iPhone Keychain. Tone hints are checked on your iPhone by a small model built into the app.

History, dictionary and settings stay on your iPhone and, if iCloud Drive is on, in your own iCloud so Parroting for Mac can use them. We cannot see your iCloud. You can delete history in the app at any time, or delete the app to remove everything.

Children. The app is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect children's data.

This website. This website (sona-orbit.github.io) uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are read. Google Analytics uses cookies and receives the pages you view, your browser and device type, and an approximate location derived from your IP address. This applies only to the website: the app does not include Google Analytics. You can block it in your browser’s privacy settings or with Google’s opt-out add-on: tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.
